Culture & Festivals

Traditional Arts

Java is famous for Batik paintings, Wayang Kulit shadow puppetry, and mesmerizing Gamelan music orchestras.

Food Paradise

Explore street foods like Nasi Goreng, Satay, Gudeg, and sweet Martabak. Your taste buds will thank you!

Festivals

Witness colorful celebrations like Sekaten Festival, Java Jazz Festival, and Vesak Day at Borobudur Temple.
