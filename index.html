Why Java?

Java, the heart of Indonesia, is an island of endless wonders. From the modern buzz of Jakarta to the ancient temples of Yogyakarta, the cool highlands of Bandung to the volcanic landscapes of Bromo, Java offers a rich tapestry of culture, nature, and adventure.
