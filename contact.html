Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Java?

The best time to visit Java is during the dry season from May to September. This period offers pleasant weather conditions with minimal rainfall, making it ideal for exploring the island's attractions.

Do I need a visa to visit Java?

Many countries are eligible for visa-free entry or visa on arrival in Indonesia for stays up to 30 days. We recommend checking the latest visa requirements based on your nationality before planning your trip.

How can I book a tour with JavaTour?

You can book our tours by filling out the contact form on this page, emailing us directly at booking@javatour.com, or calling our office at +62 123 456 7890. We'll respond within 24 hours to help arrange your perfect Java journey.

Are your tours customizable?

Absolutely! We pride ourselves on creating personalized experiences. While we offer pre-designed tour packages, we're happy to customize any aspect of the itinerary to match your preferences, interests, and schedule.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, bank transfers, and cash payments at our office. A 30% deposit is required to secure your booking.
